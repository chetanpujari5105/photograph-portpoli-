About the Artist

Rooted in Gujarat.
Drawn to the grand.

I grew up between the textile lanes of Ahmedabad and the endless white horizon of the Rann — a childhood that taught me to see both the smallest gesture and the widest sky. That tension is still my signature: the tear at a vidaai and the drone climbing over a thousand lit diyas, held in the same frame of feeling.

Over eleven years I've built a small, obsessive team of photographers, cinematographers and licensed drone pilots who treat every wedding, campaign and landscape as a film worth remembering. We shoot unhurried, we light with intention, and we deliver work that families return to for generations.
